# 🦊 V-LEARN

### Learn. Play. Compete. Earn. Grow.

V-LEARN is a gamified learning and social platform
designed to make learning interactive, personalized,
and rewarding.

The platform combines personalized quizzes, virtual
currency, team competitions, leaderboards, social
interaction, and educational resources.

Users can learn according to their interests,
participate in quizzes and competitions, earn V-Coins,
create teams, and connect with other learners.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

V-LEARN provides an interactive ecosystem where
users can learn, compete, collaborate, and earn
rewards through their participation.

The platform uses user preferences to deliver
personalized quiz experiences while encouraging
learning through gamification and community
engagement.

Users receive a unique V-ID during registration,
which acts as their personal platform identity.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🔐 1. Authentication & User Management

- User registration
- Unique V-ID generation
- Login using V-ID and password
- Google authentication
- Password recovery using mobile verification
- Password regeneration
- Secure user account management

---

### 🎯 2. Personalized Onboarding

After registration, users complete an onboarding
process where they select their interests.

Users can choose preferences such as:

- General Knowledge
- History
- Cricket
- Politics
- Technology
- Subjects
- Hobbies
- Other interests

These preferences are used to provide
personalized content and quiz recommendations.

---

### 💰 3. V-Coins Reward System

V-Coins are the internal virtual currency
of the V-LEARN platform.

Users can earn V-Coins through:

- Daily check-ins
- Winning quizzes
- Winning team competitions
- Referrals
- Other platform activities

V-Coins can also be used to access selected
platform features and educational resources.

---

### 🎮 4. Personalized Quiz System

Users receive quizzes based on their selected
preferences.

Example:

If a user selects:

- General Knowledge
- History
- Cricket
- Politics
- Technology

The platform recommends relevant quizzes.

Users can:

- Attempt quizzes
- Earn V-Coins through quiz wins
- Use limited hints
- Track quiz performance
- Improve their knowledge

---

### 🦊 5. Team Management

Users can create their own teams by spending
3,000 V-Coins.

Team features include:

- Team creation
- Member invitations
- Member approval by team creator
- Team management
- Team communication
- Team competitions

Only approved members can join the team.

---

### 💬 6. Team Chat

Team members can communicate with each other
through the team chat system.

The chat feature helps users:

- Discuss quizzes
- Coordinate competitions
- Share learning resources
- Connect with teammates

---

### 🏆 7. Leaderboard System

The leaderboard displays user and team
performance.

It includes:

- Personal performance
- Team performance
- Quiz achievements
- Competition results
- Ranking system

Leaderboards encourage healthy competition
and continuous participation.

---

### ⚔️ 8. Team Competitions

Teams can participate in competitions
against other teams.

When a team wins a competition:

- Winning team members receive V-Coins
- Competition results are recorded
- Team performance is updated
- Leaderboard rankings may change

Competition rewards will be defined according
to the specific competition rules.

---

### 💳 9. V-Coins Top-Up

Users can purchase V-Coins using real money
through Razorpay integration.

The system will support:

- Payment processing
- Payment verification
- V-Coin crediting
- Transaction records
- Payment status tracking

All transactions will be validated securely
through the backend.

---

### 📚 10. Learning Resources

Users can spend V-Coins to access selected
educational resources.

Examples include:

- Learning notes
- Study materials
- Educational content

The platform may provide free and
V-Coin-based resources.

---

### 💡 11. Quiz Hint System

Users can spend V-Coins to unlock quiz hints.

Example:

For a quiz containing 30 questions:

- Maximum 3 hints can be used
- Each hint may require V-Coins
- Hint availability depends on quiz rules

The hint system is designed to support learning
without removing the challenge of the quiz.

---

### 🤝 12. Referral System

Users can earn V-Coins by referring
new users to the platform.

The referral system will include:

- Referral tracking
- New user verification
- Reward eligibility checks
- Duplicate referral prevention
- Device-based account restrictions

The platform aims to prevent referral abuse
and fraudulent account creation.
